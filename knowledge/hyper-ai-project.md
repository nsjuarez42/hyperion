# HYPER-AI project website

HYPER-AI is an EU project. Its public site is https://hyper-ai-project.eu/.

The project works on network resource abstraction and functional registration, and on a self-protection framework.

The consortium selected five big-data processing applications, called verticals:

- Mobility and Automotive: leveraging idle computing power in automated and connected vehicles.
- Industry 4.0: AR-based and AI-augmented remote assembly, and Robot-as-a-Service, for process efficiency and worker safety.
- Healthcare: early diagnosis and control of disease to prevent future pandemics.
- Green energy: energy-efficient data-processing simulation for monitoring critical infrastructure.
- Farming and Agriculture: precision agriculture improved by a computing continuum from cloud to edge to IoT.

One of the project partners is the Technical University of Valencia (Universitat Politècnica de València, UPV).
